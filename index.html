ABOUT LA PEACE LEGIT

We are La Piece Legit, a team of young innovators participating in NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026. Through A-Pola-GIS (Analog Planetary Orbital & Landscape Analysis Geographic Information System), we aim to explore Earth’s diverse landscapes and identify terrestrial environments that share similarities with the surfaces of the Moon and Mars. By utilizing open-access data from NASA and other space agencies, we bring planetary science and geographic analysis together to discover potential terrestrial analog sites. Our goal is to transform complex planetary data into accessible visualizations and meaningful insights, helping researchers and future explorers better understand Earth’s role in preparing humanity for space exploration. Earth is not just our home. It is our testing ground for exploring other worlds.
